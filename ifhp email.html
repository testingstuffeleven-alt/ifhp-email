Important Update to Your IFHP Dental Coverage

Dear Parent/Guardian,

Starting May 1, 2026, Interim Federal Health Program (IFHP) members will be required to pay a co-payment (a portion of the cost) for certain services, including dental care. Below is a simple summary of what is changing and what it means for your family.

What is changing?

► Dental care will now require a 30% co-payment from the patient at the time of the visit
► This 30% co-payment also applies to other eligible supplemental health services and products
► Basic health care (such as doctor visits and hospital care) remains fully covered with no co-payment required

What should I do before my child's appointment?

► Ask us in advance whether a co-payment will apply to your child's planned treatment
► Confirm the expected out-of-pocket amount before receiving care so there are no surprises
► Keep your receipts for any co-payments you make at your appointments

Our team will always let you know about any co-payment that applies before your child's treatment begins. If you have any questions about what to expect at your next visit, please reach out and we will be happy to help.
